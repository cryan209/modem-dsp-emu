# Cruft K56flex client to MultiTech ISI 7910

Live investigation, 24 October 2026. Objective: establish and verify a native
cruft K56flex call to the MultiTech ISI on 7910, without permitting V.34/V.90
fallback or manufacturing negotiation/carrier state.

The answering modem is `/dev/ttyM4` on eicon420.net.cryan.nz (SSH account
scottcryan, passwordless sudo). Its identity is Lucent ISI56128_MDM Central
Site Data Modem Version 2.10V1, built 29 November 2000. `ATS109?` was 1 (both
V.90 and K56flex); changed to 0 and verified. No `AT&W` issued. `ATS108?` is
1 (A-law), `ATS38?` is 200. The PPP answer service does not reset these
registers: its chat script sends AT then ATE0V1S0=1. Stop the service before
serial inspection and restore it in a finally block; refuse an active mtppp0.

Initial PCMU and subsequent PCMA calls produce no client negotiation events.
The original client's detector waits for the complete CRe dual tone plus
single-tone segment. Incoming CRe is approximately 40 ms, followed by silence;
the client transmits silence and never reaches capability exchange.

Evidence under `artifacts/cruft-k56-7910-20261024/`:

- `probe/`: 25-second PCMU call, including the exact input fed to the client.
- `asterisk-startup.pcap`: captures both Cisco and client media legs.
- `flow0.ulaw` (Cisco -> Asterisk) equals `flow1.ulaw` (Asterisk -> caller)
  byte for byte, 189120 bytes. Both have 1182 160-byte packets with no RTP
  sequence gaps. Thus Asterisk does not truncate or transcode this signal.
- `probe-alaw/`: A-law test, still approximately 40 ms CRe. Acoustic conversion
  at the client boundary is diagnostic; not qualified for PCM data mode.
- `no-nlp/`: disabling nonlinear processing and comfort noise on Cisco voice
  port 0/0/1 does not repair CRe. Both options were restored afterward.
- `clear/`: CLEARMODE test is not equivalent: immediate answer and no modem
  carrier, consistent with UDI TA routing rather than the 3.1 kHz modem port.
- `tdm-mlog.txt`, `tdm/`: bounded physical Eicon ttyds6/controller 2 -> 7910
  V.34 call. B2 is the test leg; B1 contains another call and must not be
  attributed to this test. Unchanged B2 received A-law, decoded and repeated
  2x to 16 kHz, causes LegacyTones.listen('cre') to emit 0x3e91 at 4.336 s.
  Dual CRe is present from approximately 3.84 to 4.24 s. The complete CRe
  therefore exists on the TDM path and the original detector accepts it.

Cisco peer 6502 is the incoming VoIP peer for these calls and explicitly has
`no modem passthrough`, though the global voice service config has modem
passthrough NSE g711alaw. Voice ports 0/0/0 and 0/0/1 use A-law, no echo
canceller and no VAD. Investigating a dedicated incoming called-number 7910
peer to test passthrough without changing other call routes.

The first quick harness omitted send_bye() before hangup_call(): the latter
only signals an inbound dialog. `probe*.py` now explicitly send outgoing BYE.
Do not infer remote call clearing from local end_call alone.

## Confirmed CAPI defects and hardware progress

`Capi.send(DATA_B3, REQ, ...)` previously set the message length to include
its trailing audio. The driver's `diva_capi20_send_message` uses that field as
the payload offset and rejects `DataLength > write_length - message_length`.
Thus every generated audio request was rejected with EIO; `Gateway.pump`
only logged it at DEBUG. Fixed `capi_message` to declare 22 bytes for this
request, with its audio appended outside the message. Two added regression
tests check the driver's payload-offset/bounds contract and 160-byte CAPI
registration. All 14 gateway tests pass with localhost socket permission.
The fixed module is staged only at `/var/tmp/k56-fixed/diva_sip_gateway.py`;
the installed gateway service has not been replaced/restarted.

CAPI registration now defaults to 160-byte (20 ms) blocks instead of 2048.
The old block size caused 275 overflows in one 55.8-second call. With 160-byte
blocks there are zero overflows and one or two initial underruns per call.
The outgoing-only diagnostic gateway does not LISTEN or register to Asterisk;
it originates a CIP 4 transparent call through controller 2 to 7910.

With corrected CAPI transmit framing, `fixed-capi/` receives the ISI CL frame
`12c180808009b502009401834355e1` with valid FCS, and the native parser accepts
it as Flex (0x43) / A-law (law revision 0x61). The minimal K56Client sends its
MS but holds V.21 marking tone indefinitely and times out waiting for ACK.
A candidate host-side burst termination is diagnostic only and has not been
promoted into cruft.

The complete original native WIN95AC AT supervisor (`SerialSession` legacy)
was therefore tested through `original_at_adapter.py`. `original-at2/` uses
ATE0V1X4W1, AT+MS=K56,0, AT\\N4, AT%C0, and original ATDT7910. The original
dialer is given normal exchange dial tone and completes DTMF before placing
the physical call. The adapter uses 129-tap ADC/DAC and primes its 128-sample
PCM quantum FIFO. This is original controller execution, with no negotiation
or carrier bits supplied externally.

The original controller receives the same CL, transmits the same MS
`11c180808009b50200948181024785`, and receives valid ACK1 `14` at about 6.032 s
on the physical call audio. Its own log reports `K56 Negotiated By V8Bis`,
`buLaw = 0`, `Encoding Law: 1`, then actual K56 training and valid INFO1a.
It subsequently retrains for Data Quality Decline rather than reaching
CONNECT. There is no claim of carrier/data success.

A strict original-AT test with the previously qualified upstream cap is in
progress: `AT+MS=K56,0,300,28800,32000,56000`. Original default was 33600 up;
its MICA qualifications already distinguish unstable 31200 from 28800.

The Cisco diagnostic peer 17910 was removed. Nonlinear processing and
comfort noise on 0/0/1 were restored. The global CLEARMODE UDI mapping was
not changed (automatic review rejected a global audio-mapping experiment).

## 25 October: promoted negotiation fixes and clean training failure

The ISI has two auto-answering modem endpoints on this BRI: ttyM4 and ttyM5
both had S0=1 and both were found back at S109=1. Both were set to S109=0.
The actual PPP chat script now initializes `ATE0V1S0=1S109=0` every time it
rearms. Its prior version is backed up on the host as
`/etc/chatscripts/multitech-answer.before-flex-only-20261024`. Repository
`deploy/ppp/multitech-answer` matches. No AT&W was issued.

The controlled S108 tests restored S108=1 and S109=0 on both ttyM4 and ttyM5,
then restored the active PPP answerer. Note: even with S108=0 on both modems,
the CAPI bearer still carries A-law silence (D5), not mu-law silence. Treat
modem PCM-law signaling separately from actual bearer decoding. A mu-law
RTP/decode experiment with these A-law bytes is not a valid training control.
Changing S108 while retaining A-law bearer decoding did not establish carrier.

The minimal native client's isolated candidate now successfully completes
CRe/CRd, CL/MS/ACK1, restricted V.8 (mask 1), and starts the genuine K56 pump
against the ISI. Fixes were promoted to
`/Users/scottcryan/cruft/native/hsf/k56_client.py`:

- Wait for the rest of CRe's single-tone tail before transmitting CRd.
- Send MS as a finite V.21 carrier burst; release carrier after queue drain.
- Use a bounded message gap and base the ACK deadline on actual scheduling.
- Preserve the parsed peer's forced A-law/mu-law and Rockwell status across
  ACK parsing and pass them to the native pump instead of hardcoding mu-law
  and Rockwell.

Added cruft regressions in `tests/test_native_hsf_peer_negotiation.py` check
that the actual ISI CL fixture configures native parameter 20000=1 (A-law),
16=0 (non-Rockwell), even after the parser's ACK reset, and that a drained
message does not hold carrier indefinitely. Focused suite: 13 tests, 2
optional skips, all executed tests pass. The independent V.8bis test peer
was rebuilt from current v90modem sources into this task's artifacts, because
writing its normal cruft .work output was sandboxed.

A full MICA regression with the updated production cruft client passes:
`mica-regression.json` reports genuine K56 at 9600 upstream / 52000 downstream,
2048 upstream and 534 downstream bytes exact, zero serial framing errors,
no data interruptions, original STEADY peer state, no watchdogs. This is a
regression control, not success against the ISI.

The task's original live harness sent generated packets directly and did
not actually use EiconSipEndpoint's transmit thread/queue. It could expose
cold-pump initialization stalls to the line. The `probe_buffered.py` test
uses the existing independent wire clock, 240 ms of produced-audio buffering,
and refuses to consume an RX quantum until 160 real octets are available
(no fabricated receive silence). `buffered/summary.json` verifies zero local
TX underruns and zero RX drops; it still fails strict K56 training. Thus
that clean test, rather than earlier unbuffered calls, is the current oracle.

The minimal path also now uses exact A-law decoding/encoding at its stream
boundary (`PCMAStream` in the task harness), removing the earlier intermediate
A-law -> mu-law -> linear conversion.

`buffered/training-messages.json` shows downstream Phase-2 probing SNR about
65 dB, valid INFO1a, JD 16-point detection, Phase-3 MSE about -35 dB. The
remaining abort is initiated by the ISI roughly 2.3 seconds after the client's
HD_TRN_STARTED event; repeated attempts have the same shape. No carrier or
payload success is claimed. The strict client eventually detects internal
fallback and rejects the call, as intended. A clean 9600-upstream test is
measuring TX training target/counter to compare that timeout to the genuine
TRN duration; do not shorten firmware training by poking internal state.

Native K56 diagnostic `%f` arguments are pointers to float32 (confirmed at
recovered 12022ec0), not standard printf doubles. `printf_trace.py` decodes
that K56 ABI; earlier experimental double-decoded values in
`mu-map-alaw-bearer/` are invalid and must not be used as measurements.

Gateway regression suite now has 15 passing tests, including explicit PCMU
RTP/silence/bearer-octet behavior. `--law` defaults to pcma and never
transcodes. The installed persistent service remains unchanged; only the
isolated module at `/var/tmp/k56-fixed/` is used for hardware tests.
